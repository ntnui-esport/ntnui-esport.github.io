**# NTNUI E-Sport Inhouse Bot

The Discord bot for **NTNUI E-Sport**, the esports group of NTNUI, the student sports club at NTNU in Trondheim, Norway.

## What it does
- **Inhouse games:** members queue in Discord and the bot makes balanced 5v5 teams for League of Legends, Valorant and Overwatch.
- **League draft:** captains ban and pick champions together inside Discord.
- **Match tracking:** results, match history and simple stats for our members.
- **Club life:** event reminders, volleyball sign-ups, support tickets and more.

## Riot Games API
For League of Legends the bot uses the official Riot API to look up players' rank when they register, and tournament codes so players can join the right custom lobby directly. Region: EUW.

All games are free. There are no entry fees, prizes or real-money betting.
